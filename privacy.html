Prism Burst Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Prism Burst is published by KINDIX DEVS. Contact: kindixdevs@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your scores, coins, level progress, badges, settings and purchase status are saved on your own device. We do not run a server that receives them, and we do not ask you to create an account.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts or precise location.

Ads

The game shows banner ads, occasional full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded video ads (you choose to watch them to earn a reward), all provided by Google AdMob. Subscribing to Premium removes the banner and full-screen ads. Google AdMob and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and usage information to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) the game asks for your consent first. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. Learn more: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

If you buy coins, remove ads or subscribe to Premium, the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see your card details. Google Play tells the game that a purchase or subscription is active so the game can deliver it. Subscriptions renew until you cancel them in Google Play.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions: kindixdevs@gmail.com.
